Barakos' father made him a metal dragon chess set when he was younger, and it was one of the things that inspired him to start creating things. When Inyvir was a child, Barakos gave the set to her and she became very good at playing very quickly. After the whole shenanigans with Barakos being enlisted and his family being captured, the main thing Inyvir has to remember him by is the dragon chess set. Then if you do the whole she's alive and indoctrinated into being a soldier bit, you could do this. When she's got the enemy surrounded or defeated, she sits their leader down and commands them to play dragon chess with her. As an officer she is a strategic genius and chooses to use dragon chess to read people. What kind of opening do they play? Are they aggressive or defensive? Risky or safe? How do they perform under pressure? I don't know how you'd plan on depicting Inyvir but I imagine her starting off as being dedicated yet strange, causing her fellow soldiers to pick on her. Then once she becomes an officer, she becomes very aggressive and assertive so that even her peers fear her - although she remains fiercely loyal to Ira. I'm imagining a scene where she finds Barakos, captures him and forces him to sit down and play dragon chess with her, with him recognising the specific set immediately. He's distressed and constantly trying to find ways to appeal to her humanity, but she remains insistent and tries to get a read on her father. Ultimately, he disappoints her. Later on (this a whole dynamic and eventually other scene btw) she continues to try to test him as she hopes to reconnect with him, but wouldn't dare betray Ira. So, as Barakos would ideally have a dad-like relationship with at least one of the PCs (or the group as a whole) she tries to make him choose between them or him.
