# ![[120292357-Carric-Yernuant.jpeg|300]] Backstory;

Carric grew up in an ancient civilisation of elves, her home life was, to put it nicely, unfortunate. Her people were rather quick to violence, taking part in many wars over the years, and often members of the military took this mindset home with them, much to the unpleasant childhood of Carric. When she reached an age where she could start training her family forced her into the military, learning to fight with a spear and shield. Carric fought this, trying to encourage peace where possible. After basic training they tried to make her swear an oath as a cleric to a war god, but the pledge never took hold during her time in her sect. Eventualy she grew tired of the constant pressure and effective harrassment from her family and peers and chose to defect, running from her home and attempting to convince her love at the time to leave with her, he however was devout to the cause and remained behind as she left With time Carric started to use her skills in order to help those in need, taking up peacekeeping jobs in towns as she passed through, eventually being visited by an emisary of Angharradh, the elven god of peace, this time however the pledge took hold, she had found her divine calling, swearing that nobody else would experience what she had in her youth. Although as strong willed as she is, the constant compulsion to encourage those around her to peaceful resolutions and the loss of the one she loved has lead to her to a habit of drinking during her off hours, to quench the turmoil that she fends off within her

(Lead to [[Ares]] Becoming a Lawful Good god, and subsequently spread that to their followers)
