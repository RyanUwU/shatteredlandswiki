# ![[zia_s_character.Avatar.webp|300]] Backstory;

As a sea elf ranger, I hail from a proud lineage that once served as the Queen's esteemed confidantes. However, my past is marred by scandal and betrayal. I was once the Queen's favored mistress, my desires led me astray from my position in the court, causing me to steal the affections of the King's paramour.

This treacherous act led to my banishment from the kingdom, severing my ties to the royal court. Despite my fall from grace, I remain loyal to the memory of the Queen, who exuded a fragrance reminiscent of roses.

In my youth, I formed an unlikely bond with a mischievous frog named Skrunk. We made a pact that he would accompany me on my journeys as long as I provided him with sustenance. However, Skrunk's appetite is unique—he craves only the most valuable of treasures. Thus, I find myself embarking on quests to procure riches to appease his voracious appetite.

My upbringing echoes that of a classic tale of loss and resilience. Both of my parents perished when I was young, leaving me to be raised within the confines of a castle as a lady-in-waiting. Yet, as I matured, I yearned for adventure beyond the confines of courtly life, eventually mastering the art of the bow and embracing the wilds as my true home.

Also I fucked the Queen, If it wasn't obvious.
