# ![[aikran.AvatarSlY08Ot1d3Q5wXFe.webp]]Backstory;

Raised to trust his God undoubtfully, he isn't aware of the corruption his God is manipulating him into. His blind trust in his God results in him not suspecting that anything wrongful could be happening.

Aikran was raised in a monestery, training his whole life to become the paladin his parents wanted him to be. He spent hours training and studying scripture of his God to show his devotion and faith. His congregation saw his slow decent into corruption by the hand of his God and banished him from the monastery. His God, however, saw this as a form of betrayal and commanded Aikran to burn down the building. Many lives were lost but Aikran believes fully that it was rightful act from the Hand of God.

(God in Question is [[Ares]])
