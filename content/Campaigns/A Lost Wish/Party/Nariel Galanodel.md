# ![[120291397-Nariel-Galanodel.jpeg]] Backstory;

Nariel Galanodel was born in a secluded elven village nestled deep within a dense and mysterious forest. However, the tranquility of his childhood was shattered when troubles erupted between the elves and dark forces seeking to corrupt the forest. His village was ravaged, and the survivors scattered. Nariel, still very young, found himself alone in the abandoned ruins.

In those ruins, he found an old dusty grimoire that had once belonged to a renowned elven mage. Hungry for knowledge and thirsting for vengeance for his lost village, Nariel spent years studying the teachings of the book. He learned the secrets of magic and nature, as well as the skills necessary to survive in the wilds.

Guided by his thirst for justice and his bond with nature, Nariel forged a new identity as a solitary guardian of the forest, swearing to protect his natural home against any threat that dared to present itself.
