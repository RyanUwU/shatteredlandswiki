# **Backstory:**

In the ethereal realms of Faerie, Echo was born to parents of high standing, showered with opulence and extravagance from the moment she took her first breath. Raised in the lap of luxury, she grew accustomed to having her every whim catered to, her every desire fulfilled. But amidst the shimmering splendor of her fairy home, Echo's heart grew restless and her spirit yearned for something more.

One fateful day, Echo, accompanied by her retinue of maids, embarked on a leisurely excursion through the enchanted forests of Faerie. Yet, in a moment of carelessness and distraction, she strayed from the familiar paths, venturing deeper into the mystical woods than she had ever dared before. Lost amidst the tangled foliage and whispered secrets of the forest, Echo found herself ensnared in the throes of the material plane.

Confused and disoriented, Echo emerged from the shadowy depths of the forest into a world far removed from her own. Towering trees gave way to sprawling cities, and the gentle melodies of the fae were drowned out by the cacophony of mortal life. Determined to find her way back home, Echo realized that her privileged upbringing had left her ill-prepared for the harsh realities of this unfamiliar realm.

With a stubborn resolve born of desperation, Echo set out to navigate the unfamiliar landscape, her once haughty demeanor tempered by the harsh lessons of adversity. Casting aside her former entitlement, she embraced the pragmatism of survival, determined to carve out a place for herself in this strange new world. Armed with nothing but her wits and a burning desire to return to her fairy kingdom, Echo embarked on a quest for wealth and fortune, knowing that only by amassing enough riches could she hope to find her way back home.
