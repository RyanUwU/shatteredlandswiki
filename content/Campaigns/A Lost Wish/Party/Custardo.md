
# ![[custardo_the_wise.Avatar.webp|300]] Backstory;

Custardo was originally just a humble hawkman who did his due dilligence in learning from the elders of his village. One day he stumbled across a gnome who promised him great knowledge even the elders had never known. He promised knowledge of the gods the elders spoke only in legends about, all their powers and even a chance to meet them. He lost his soul in this deal which now leaves him unable to make social connections with anything that can speak common, and will only make stronger social connections to those that can speak to him in another language. He now seeks out redemption for his deal to regain his soul and social skills, however this was only promised to return to him once he had experienced all the feelings of friendship; love, loss, worry, joy, banter and accomplishment. Only once he had made a final wish and felt all 6 of these feelings whilst being seemingly unable to understand them, would he be able to return to the social ways of his youth.
