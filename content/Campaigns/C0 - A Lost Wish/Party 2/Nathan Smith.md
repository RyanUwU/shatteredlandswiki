# ![[nathan_smith.Avatar.webp|300]] Backstory;
When growing up in the village, they admired a girl (Emily) that loved tending to the horses on their family's farm. Nathan would sneak over to go see them and help out. Eventually, Emily revealed who they liked was very different to the current scrawny kid that they were seeing and so after years of training and becoming reliable they went back, only to find that Emily had already found love.

Nathan then found his now dog [[Ginger]] the Golden Retriever and has been trying to make ends meet for his new buddy, and trying to get friendly with the local butcher.
