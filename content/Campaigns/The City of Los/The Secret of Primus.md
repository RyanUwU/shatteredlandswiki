# THE SECRET OF PRIMUS

The big secret I want to keep behind the whole setting is that Primus was never actually supposed to be created.

The original plan was to build the Mechanus as a prison.

The Modrons were supposed to maintain it.

But as the Modrons became more complex, they began developing concepts of order, perfection, hierarchy and purpose.

Eventually, they started asking questions.

**Who created us?**

**Why were we created?**

**What is the purpose of order?**

And eventually:

**Who is above us?**

The researchers gave them an answer.

> **Primus.**

There was no Primus.

Not yet.

But millions of minds believing in something is potentially enough to create something.

The Modrons begin worshipping the idea.

The Divine Lattice responds.

The Mechanus changes.

And somewhere deep within the machine, something begins to wake up.

The city thinks it is building Primus.

It might actually be **discovering him.**

# THE TRAGEDY OF LOS

Los was created to save civilisation from Hellus.

But every step towards completing the Mechanus makes the city more authoritarian, more centralised and more obsessed with efficiency.

The city becomes increasingly willing to sacrifice individuals for the greater good.

It becomes more dependent upon surveillance.

More dependent upon artificial beings.

More dependent upon magical energy.

More dependent upon questionable experiments.

More dependent upon the very systems it originally intended to use temporarily.

Los is slowly becoming more like the thing it fears.

And nobody really notices.

Because every time someone raises an objection, the answer is:

> **"People are dying. We do not have time for this."**
