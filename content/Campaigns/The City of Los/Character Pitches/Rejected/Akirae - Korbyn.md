An archaeologist and historian, Akirae is driven by an adoration for both the past and present alike. Originally drifting across the continent in search of its next discovery or occasionally being hired as an archivist for libraries and governments, through it’s study Akirae came to realise that while the past remains set, the present is more malleable than one might assume, leading it towards the study of Chronurgy as another facet of it’s research. Despite the nature of it’s work, Akirae never gave much thought to the future, preferring to simply catalogue events and discussions as they come. 

This relatively carefree outlook was readily shattered on the advent of the invasion, with such destruction clearly spelling out that Akirae’s precious records would soon come to a screeching halt should nothing be done. Upon hearing of the unification and effort to contain the Hells’ weapon, it travelled to the city of Los in order to provide whatever assistance it could in an effort to defend the creation of the past it holds so dear.

Main Attributes 
- Socially Underperforming: a life comprised of solo travel and archiving documents has led to a rather noticeable lack of skill when interacting with others, mainly amounting in an inability to small talk and a tendency to cut off conversations abruptly once it has decided it is done with whoever it’s talking to. 
- Astute Archivist: despite lack of skill in discussing, Akirae possesses great ability to record and cross reference conversations it is privy to using a small leather notebook that also works double as it’s spellbook. This tendency to catalogue makes Akirae surprisingly difficult to lie to more than once.
- Morally Neglectful: Through it’s discoveries and documenting (particularly of government records) Akirae has learned that laws and morals are only an obstacle if the one enforcing them wishes them to be, and as such is not above dipping into illegality to get at what it’s after, as long as it’s sure it won’t get caught.
