City Of Los ea Character backstory: 

Darren Glister 

Backstory: 

Darren came from a small town upon the shore of the town Zhathi of Biok where Darren learned about hard work, labour and how to tell what's right from what's wrong. He never learnt this from his family though, but from the people who helped him in his times of struggle. 

His family on the other hand were a group of fellow fish folk (sharks) who consistently belittled and tormented him. Mainly his two brothers often picked on him for his weakness, his mother on his pathetic mind and his father on his cowardice. Portraying Darren as a disappointment and embarrassment not only to the Glister name but the whole race.  

Due to this, Darren spent a long amount of his days above the water and helping the public instead of terrorising like his family. Fixing their mistakes like damaged property and injuring people. Staying by the sides of the people that he could truly call family. 

And not long, he would be known by many in the town to be loved and not feared. Putting others Infront of himself. And working for an old scrap yard ran by a woman named Shellsy, she was 25 years old and kept the family scrap business.  Darren would often suggest his help, but he never wanted anything for it besides to talk to Shellsy.  

For days and days, he would offer his help, delivering and transporting the large amounts of scrap between the town. The town in which he found good people. Good people who he called family. And Shellsy, the person he would die for. 

Yet he was not there when he was truly needed.  

On the afternoon of the day that he was sent to do a large delivery he saw the tragedy of a nightmare he wishes would never come true. 

Blood, guts, fur, clothes everything splattered across the floor. Then, he knew it was them; his family were to blame he could feel it. Running and running, crying out her name to try his best to find her. Corner after corner. Crevasses and rubble he helped and searched till a gruelling scream came north of him. A recognisable scream he heard getting louder as he ran. 

Spotting Shellsy over a fence he rams through it just to see the most horrifying sight 

Ahead of him hung Shellsy from what appears to be a large fishhook with her lower half severed and both lungs punctured. The final scream that released from her dying breath was so gut curling and gruelling that she continued to scream even after death.   

Darren watched in disbelief as the not only the town but his closest friend, the one in which he loved and cherished the most, be dealt with the most torturous death.  Causing an unexplainable rage and need for justice build up inside him. As he slides the corpse off the hook and drags said hook towards the cost, leaving a red streak behind him. 

He find the fish he so called his father and rushed without saying a word. Swinging the very weapon his father used against Shellsy. Swinging back and forth the hook in which he couldn't control, resulting in knocking a tooth out of himself.  

Lurking above him his father uttered: 

'looks like the only thing you gained from this family is the blood in which you let flow away' 

As he walks off before letting Darren reply. Left there to think about what he's done. 

After a while ,Darren began to rebuild to the best of his ability and yet could fix what was truly broken. Lugging around many scraps and more throughout the time. Waiting to issue revenge
