Nasir was born into the pureblooded drow family of Crafina, raised in a community where he was taught that his lineage put him at a higher status than those of the other species that live in the Underdark. He never understood why something as simple as the circumstances in which he was born meant that he was somehow more important, so as soon as he was trusted to leave his house alone, roughly at the age of 10, in secret, he would traverse to areas of the outer city to speak to the other races that lived in the area.

His mother (Haramara) and father (Elion) were of a middle status amongst the drow, though this still made them high ranking in the overall status of the underdark. They both worked as tax collectors, meaning they would be the ones to traverse to the outer city and chase up any families who tried to avoid paying the extortionate amount of taxes. This often resulted in dinners consisting of them complaining about the people living in the outer city and how they should "simply live better lives if they didn't want to be poor".

Fortunately, his parents were rarely home for dinner, meaning he spent most of his time with the staff who kept the house running. His parents, not wanting to seem "unkind to the less fortunate" hired some of those who lived in the outer city, not that they paid them much for the "exclusive opportunity". Nasir could never understand his parents bigotry towards the people in the outer city, and that wouldn't change as he gets older.

During his time sneeking out to the outer city, he became rather familiar with many of the inhabitants of the outer city, mainly a dwarven woman, Krakhina Coinriver, who ran a simple repair business with her husband, Fononlim Coinriver . When he first met them, he couldn't pronounce their names so she told him to just call them Hina and Fonon.

Hina never discriminated against him because of his drow lineage, often giving him bread and other sweet treats that she had spare for free. She spent her free time when he was there educating him on how life was like for her and the other races living in the outer city, explaining how the drow place such heavy taxes on them (she had to explain what taxes were) that many days, poorer families went without food, all their money being spent on having a place to live because on the streets was far worse. He tried giving her some money that he'd stolen from his parents a couple times, though she never accepted it.

As Nasir got older, she would go into more detail for the curious child. She would explain why he should avoid certain streets she warned him about when he was younger, explaining the violence and thefts that occurred for the sake of survival.

At his request, she would teach him how to repair simple mechanical devices, teaching him why certain parts go where and what purpose each piece had in the final product. He began helping her out in the workshop whenever he snuck out, which became more often as he got older, his parents not caring as much about his whereabouts.

Sometime after his 27th birthday, he was visiting Hina and Fonon when an explosion goes off somewhere in the distance. He was quick to follow the Coinriver's outside to see what the commotion was about. After seeing fire and smoke in the distance, quickly approaching as more explosions go off, Hina ushers him inside towards the back of the workshops to a small hatch. She hands him a small bag , which she explains has food, water, and a shawl to cover himself with inside and forces him into the small crawlspace that leads to a tunnel. At her insistence, he squeezes himself through, fear blooming in him at the possible state of his home and the people he cares about but not wanting to disobey Hina, he carries on (not that he could turn back even if he wanted to, the crawlspace obviously carved out for the size of a dwarf).

He hears the explosions practically on top of the shop, hears the screams of those who cared for him. He feels the tears streaming down his face as he continues on, the crawl space feels smaller as he cries, his anxiety screaming at him as he pushes through, begging to himself to just keep going. He doesn't know how long he crawls for, whether it's days or merely hours, his arms and legs burning with ache. At some point, he catches his thigh on a sharp rock which tears a large gash through his skin. He wants to stop, wants to check on his wound but he has to keep going, he has too, Hina told him not to stop until he saw light.

By the time he does eventually see light, he feels weak, whether it's from exhaustion or blood lose, he is unsure. All he knows, is that he's somewhere above ground (he later finds out is the Republic of Combria. in the town of Ignee) and the sun is burning at his skin, with which he's quick to dig the shawl out of the pack and shield himself from the light.

He finds a small alleyway to hide in while he checks his leg and eats something. The gash on his legs is rather large, while the bleeding has slowed he does still feel the effects of the blood lose. He tries his best to clean it and cover it with whatever he can find in the pack, which turns out to be some scrap fabrics which were definitely not clean enough to be used as a bandage.

During the time he waits for his leg to heal, he steals scraped food from bins, lives in alleys, and tries his hardest to survive, for Hina and Fonon. He steals any mechanical scraps he can find, tinkering with them just to create something, longing for some resemblance of normality.

Once his leg has healed enough that he can move around a bit, he starts breaking into places, searching for better food than that from the rubbish as well as anything he can get his hands on to sell. After a few weeks he manages to save up enough for him to move on from Ignee, hiring a travelling merchant to take him as far as he could, ending up in Rearare, where he continues honing his skills in breaking and entering.

About a month after his 28th birthday, he finds a house on more of the side of middle class and spends a few days scoping it out, figuring out the perfect time to break in. He finds out that the man who owns the house is leaving for a trip for a couple days, giving Nasir ample opportunity to take anything he wishes (but not enough to trace it back to him).

It's late at night when he breaks in, picking the lock on the door with ease. As he's looking around the house, picking up a few small items that show to have some form of value, he runs into someone, someone who also isn't supposed to be there.
