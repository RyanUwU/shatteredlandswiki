# Backstory:

The City of Los was never his original home. Born and passed around, a child that could never fit in wherever he tried. All of his life, he was seen as a fuse just waiting to catch light. And often it did. Families that were cautious that tried to leave him to his own devices would find burnt grass or outdoor furniture catching a light. Other families saw them as an opportunity, someone warm to sit by only being loved by the warmth they could provide during cold months. With the variety of people around him, they were quick to pick up on their mannerisms, when they would lie, or when someone was hiding their true intentions. Making it all the more difficult to find someone to trust, when half their face is plastered with porcelain.

  

Only when they found purpose did they practise, being innately attuned to the primordial nature of fire gave them a point to start from, and after a few years they turned the thing people were afraid of into a bargaining chip to find his place in the world. At first he got a job in construction at the age of 16, thanks to being able to light the cigar from under an old builder's mouth that was notorious for having a broken lighter and being too cheap to buy a new one, his name was Alabaster. After building up his strength, through tired eyes with time to kill, he received a longsword from a retired adventurer named Cliffe Warrick. The blade was dull and needed work, but it was a start.Yuki kept it safe and even asked the local blacksmith to repair it in exchange for fixing part of their wall that was affecting the temperature of the forge. Until the age of 18, where Alabaster became the boss and let him go to encourage Yuki to find a new line of work, because “a growing boy shouldn’t throw their back out at 30”.

  

Now without an income and no home to return to, he had to find a new source. So went to Cliffe, the adventurer that had given them the sword, and after returning it in a better condition than it was left in they were shocked to see Yuki still had it. Lessons weren’t free, due to a coming baby in the family and having an extra source of income to prepare can never hurt, which Yuki agreed to, as he had enough saved thanks to favours and free lunches. 

  

There were 2 things Yuki learned while under his mentor. How to keep a blade clean and sharp, and that fighting was all about tempo. So training was done outside the blacksmith’s workshop, where Yuki could aspire to be one of the many adventurers that walked in looking for something new and used the sound of the hammer fall as the metronome of his fighting style. 7 months passed and Yuki’s mentor had their baby, their priorities were focused elsewhere and lessons were postponed indefinitely, and by that time he was 19, he found courage to find a party. Finding a group of adventurers to join that needed a light to guide their way was easy, at a tavern, he met a group consisting of 2 Humans, a Dragonborn and a Harengon, so having someone to light the way gave them hope.

  

One of the human’s, Brandon Westwoods, was nervous on my arrival, at first I thought it was nerves, but their eyes were focused, as if they were analysing me, planning and scheming. It was safe to say that I did not feel safe and stayed on overnight watches with them, and I was right to be afraid. One night, everyone heard movement and a scream, the fire was out so no one could see, except for Yuki. Brandon had murdered his Dragonborn companion. Acting fast, using their magic, Yuki revealed Brandon’s crimes and was swiftly dealt with. They received thanks from the rest of the group after returning home with the quest completed, but that had meant he was alone again.

  

A few days passed and the Harengon, Briar Keenloft, found Yuki again and asked to join them to go to the next town over. From there the two worked together and formed a group, named Prism’s Edge. They became well known and had worked their way up together, and while some members changed here and there, the group became well known for the next decade. Yuki was never a leader, and he wasn’t for the longest time, that mantle was owned by Jackson, he never gave us his last name, never needed to, but one day he left, no warning no note. Just a lot of questions. With the group nowhere to go, Yuki made a decision to take a quest North from xxxxxxx. That was the worst decision he ever made.

  

The road was rough, colder than where they travelled before, the group was unprepared. But Yuki thought things would be fine, he would be the fire, both warm and light to guide the way the group needed. But all it took was for them to be away for a moment. Briar, while scouting ahead, sent a signal that they were in danger, as she lay at the feet of a Wyvern. Everyone sprung into action, but all Yuki could think of was, “Why is it here? Now of all times!” All it took was a moment of distraction, where Briar was about to be eaten, that Yuki had to act and moved in the way of the bite, shield arm ready as his left arm came clean off, severing at the shoulder. It was strong, much stronger than it should have been, unprepared and the group afraid. Yuki barely managed to survive.

  

They couldn’t go back now, after resting for a while, they pushed forward to the City of Los. There they could rest and plan, but Yuki was distraught and weak. To the point where Yuki determined that they were of no use. Briar was sad to part and felt guilty for their mistake and got them a present, a shield that floated around Yuki, protecting them. It was also meant to be used to encourage Yuki to continue adventuring, but he fell into despair not long after Briar left.

  

Thankfully, money was fruitful and he had enough to live for a while, learn about this new city that was meant to be revolutionary. Yuki took it a bit too literally and thought it may have included fighting, but the mechanical marvel astounded him more than any fight could. But he needed to find his place, and so went to the only place he could find comfort, a Blacksmiths. There he met Keegan McGernoff, a Dwarven master of metal and welding, there he learned how he worked until he thought he had gotten the hang of it, but it took time. And after 5 years learning from them, eventually the mantle being passed to him, and while Keegan stuck around as he lived above the forge, it was now a place that Yuki could call home.

  

10 Years passed as the city became even more popular, Keegan was still upstairs and helped where he could (mostly with his own projects) but the problem lied in Yuki’s intuition, turning down crooks and criminals, liars that tried to swindle, but they were the only business Yuki could get when the City flies through the sky, and now needs a new set of income.
