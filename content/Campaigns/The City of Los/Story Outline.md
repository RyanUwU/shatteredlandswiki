The Lords of Hellus have built and used "the spine" an unholy superweapon that destroyed the home of Celestia's eldest children and took the majority of the shadow moon, now the shadar kai unite the elven factions under one purpose beyond conquest, the subjugation of Hellus under a ruler of their creation.
In this short campaign your characters are enlisted upon the split city of Los, to construct a device to imprison the hells at the end of the last great war, the city is full of the greatest minds of Drusus, and will regularly fly to and from different continents to acquire them and necessary resources. But be warned the Hells will not roll over so easily, corruption and avarice plague the city, the hells last ditch attempt to prevent the creation of Mekanus and the god Primus

This is a 6-8 month campaign, 24 sessions, 7.30pm to 11pm 84 hours of story.

# **Table Expectations**
**❏ Distractions, multi-tasking and engagement;** doing other menial tasks that are *genuinely* mindless, and help you focus is allowed, but if at any point I or another of the players feel as though your attention is dedicated somewhere other than the campaign, you will be asked to either pay attention or leave the 'table'.

❏ Reviewing a ruling; preferably only a few seconds, or up to a couple minutes speaking amongst ourselves. But in the case of no clear, *immediate* answer either between us or online from a brief search, I can and will make a ruling based off what I feel is best for the scene given the actions occurring.

❏ Player knowledge & overlaps with character knowledge; Specifically for this campaign (The City of Los), your knowledge as players *should* be exactly the same as your characters, but as a general rule of thumb, ive broken character knowledge down for simplicity;

- If your character hasn't encountered a thing or topic before - they are unlikely to know anything about it (Duh)
- A brief experience with a thing or topic - Will likely have an opinion about it but their information is likely incorrect
- A recorded history with a thing or topic - For when a character has spent time within an environment rich with a thing or topic, they are likely to know things about this that others dont.
- A Core part of their life revolves around a thing or topic - IE the Tiefling raised in hell KNOWS about hell (Duh)
	These generalisations are for NON roll based RPing, ignore all of this if I say "Roll a history check" or something like that
  
❏ Preferred depth of roleplay; I don't plan on making yall stay in character **all the time** but this campaign is very narratively focused so understanding and embodying your character and knowing how they would respond or feel to whatever is going on in a scene is important.

❏ General tone; Dramatic Sci-Fa Mystery

❏ All characters get time in the spotlight if they want it!

❏ Player narrative control; the main narrative of the campaign **is** unavoidable, but, when characters aren't in a situation that would prescribe their full attention Players are free to have them seek out interests or other plot points at their own will.

❏ Take backs, or retconning, will be permitted when;
- the DM misrepresents a scene or creature.
- a player describes their character’s actions as an obvious joke.
- before the player rolls.

❏ Player-vs-player action is acceptable;
❏ during social interactions but never during combat unless charmed or under some kind of effect

❏ Discuss any RPG pet peeves that have not yet been addressed, and if the subject matter
becomes serious, go immediately to the next point.

● Note: Do not underestimate the importance of this point for keeping a group together!
Request that your players privately share anything they do not want to occur in the game,
and determine another signal if they would not be comfortable saying “please stop”
during the game.
❏ Discuss the consequences for breaking these rules.
● Note: Consequences are required to keep players from abusing your group’s agreements
and spoiling the game for your more dedicated players. A simple disapproving look will

not be a good fit for your game group.

House Rules
❏ Alignment: determines actions & changed by repeated actions;
As RP focused campaign your alignment is a guide to how you behave, 
i.e a lawful evil character follows the rules of agreements and keeps promises, but is likely to add clauses that are self serving or intentionally vague
or a chaotic good character doesnt have a specific code or creed but believes in doing the right thing in the moment.
this wont be *enforced* as people often act outside of these generalisations often, BUT if you repeatedly act outside of your alignment, it will change and you will be encouraged to adjust your view and RP style to that new attitude.

for example, if a lawful good paladin breaks their oath, and continues to fall from their tenants, they will likely change to chaotic good,
or a chaotic evil character starts to make bargains and follow the rules of devils and demons, they will likely shift to lawful evil

❏ Backgrounds: pre-generated, custom, not used, other.
❏ Encumbrance: F that, unless I look at your characters inventory and go "WTF HOW MUCH STUFF DO YOU HAVE" idrc

❏ Critical success: double dice, not used, other.
❏ Critical failure: fumble, not used, other.
❏ Inspiration: I will be using my custom inspiration called "Divinity" (See Divinity Rules)
❏ Number of skill check attempts, group skill checks, other.
❏ Initiative: highest roll plus Dexterity, around the table, other.
❏ Starting gold: class-based, other.
❏ Resting, health regeneration, other.
❏ Death, resurrection: as written, other.
