Suar Nach was born into the Aber'h Tribe, and she had a penchant for theft from a young age. Despite her innate ability, tribe members attempted to dissuade her from choosing a criminal career. Her older brother Amen Nach, an experienced thief and member of the local thieves' guild, spotted her potential and persuaded her to join him. They began pulling daring heists together, stealing from affluent merchants and even their own tribe's precious relics.

When they were charged with stealing an important tribal artefact from their own kin, their luck ran out. Suar Nach had reservations about taking the job, but Amen told her that it was for the sake of the tribe. After they got the item, however, Amen betrayed Suar by selling it to a wealthy collector for a large sum of money, leaving her to bear the blame for the theft.

Suar was apprehended and expelled from the tribe, and she soon found herself travelling south, hopping from city to city, seeking to hide from justice. It didn't take long for her to fall in with the wrong crowd and start working for the criminal underground. It was there that she met Kami, a little but powerful red panda who became her close confidante and underworld liaison.

Suar's thievery caught up with her, despite her efforts to remain hidden. She was eventually apprehended stealing from a wealthy noble in one of the cities she passed through. Despite Kami's efforts to assist her escape, Suar was eventually apprehended and imprisoned. The evidence against her was overwhelming, and there appeared to be no way out of her predicament. As she sat in her cell awaiting execution, she was moved to an alternate cell for "capacity problems," where she was paired with a large copper skin dragon born as her cellmate.

---

After a vastly dramatized escape from prison these two cell mates became close friends and shared a few adventures together, including, but not limited to, geting abducted by a necromancer so he can use her body to revive his dead wife, before going their separate ways.

Suar now reflects fondly of her memories with one "Lump Beef Broth" and their traveling companion "Ianren"
