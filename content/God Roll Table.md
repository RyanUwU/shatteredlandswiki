# 🎲 Pantheon & Kami Roll Tables

## 1. Tier & Origin
**Roll Tier:** `dice: 1d12` *(If you roll 11, roll Ascendant Tier: `dice: 1d8`)*

| 1d12 | Tier & Origin | Worldbuilding Hook |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1–4** | **Shrine God** (Town/Settlement) | Born from a hyper-local superstition, tavern toast, or village landmark. |
| **5–7** | **Lesser God** (Regional/Cultural) | Worshipped across a specific kingdom, trade route, or cultural sub-group. |
| **8–9** | **Planar God** (World/Continent) | A household name across the continent with formal temples and clerics. |
| **10** | **Greater God** (Hellus-Abyssus System) | Cosmic force known across multiple planes and worlds. |
| **11** | **Ascendant** *(Roll 1d8 below)* | A mortal whose deeds or rumours grew so exaggerated that belief made them divine. |
| **12** | **Demigod / Aasimar** | Born from a mortal pact with a Shrine/Lesser god, or a Planar+ god "cosplaying Zeus". |

### Ascendant Tier (1d8)

| 1d8 | Ascendant Tier |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1–4** | Shrine Tier |
| **5–6** | Lesser Tier |
| **7** | Planar Tier |
| **8** | Greater Tier |

## 3. The "Belief Engine" Quirk
**Roll Quirk:** `dice: 1d12`

| 1d12   | Belief Twist               | Campaign Plot Hook                                                                                                                                            |
| :----- | :------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1**  | **Accidental Ascension**   | A living tavern bard or folk hero is slowly turning into an **Ascendant** because exaggerated stories about them are spreading too fast—and they hate it.     |
| **2**  | **Misunderstood Doctrine** | A **Lesser** god of something peaceful was accidentally misinterpreted by a violent mercenary company, forcing the god to gain War powers against their will. |
| **3**  | **Fading Shrine**          | Only one elderly NPC still remembers this **Shrine** god's true name. If they die, the god ceases to exist.                                                   |
| **4**  | **Rival Takeover**         | Two neighbouring towns worship nearly identical **Shrine** gods of the same river, and the gods are actively trying to merge or absorb each other.            |
| **5**  | **Cosmic Spillover**       | A **Shrine** god accidentally tapped into the power of **The Sona** or **The Shai-het**, mutating their local blessings into strange life/undeath magic.      |
| **6**  | **Zeus Fallout**           | A **Planar** god's mortal offspring (Demigod) has no idea who their divine parent is, yet a cult has already formed around the kid.                           |
| **7**  | **Branding Crisis**        | A **Lesser** god is desperately trying to rebrand their portfolio to attract more worshippers before a rival steals their territory.                          |
| **8**  | **Object Anchor**          | A **Shrine** god's entire existence is tied to a mundane item (like a specific brass kettle or bridge), and someone just stole it.                            |
| **9**  | **Forced Pacifism**        | Like **Ares**, a terrifying monster or warlord became so associated with ending wars that belief forced them into becoming a god of peace.                    |
| **10** | **Cult Inflation**         | A joke religion started by bored sailors actually worked, accidentally spawning a brand-new **Shrine** god on their ship.                                     |
| **11** | **Heretical Split**        | A **Planar** god is suffering from a literal split personality because two continents worship completely different versions of them.                          |
| **12** | **Celestia's Audit**       | **Celestia** (Overgod) has intervened because a minor god found a loophole to farm belief artificially.                                                       |

## 6. Offerings & Taboos
**Roll Offering:** `dice: 1d12` | **Roll Taboo:** `dice: 1d12`

| 1d12   | Favoured Offering                           | Taboo (Angers the God)                          |
| :----- | :------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------- |
| **1**  | Crisp rice wine (sake) poured on stone      | Spilling blood near the shrine                  |
| **2**  | Folded paper talismans tied to branches     | Whistling or shouting after dark                |
| **3**  | Fresh river fish or seasonal fruit          | Stepping on the threshold stone                 |
| **4**  | Hand-copied poems, songs, or flute music    | Telling a lie within sight of the altar         |
| **5**  | Polished river pebbles stacked in cairns    | Wearing iron weapons or armour inside           |
| **6**  | Bundles of sweet incense or cedar smoke     | Turning your back before bowing twice           |
| **7**  | A single copper coin tossed into water      | Taking anything from the grove (even a twig)    |
| **8**  | Red cloth ribbons or woven straw ropes      | Eating four-legged meat before praying          |
| **9**  | Painted wooden plaques with written wishes  | Approaching with muddy boots or unwashed hands  |
| **10** | Sweet rice cakes or roasted chestnuts       | Speaking the god's true name aloud              |
| **11** | A lit candle kept burning through the night | Refusing hospitality to a stranger nearby       |
| **12** | Tears or a lock of hair given in sincerity  | Pointing a finger directly at the sacred object |
